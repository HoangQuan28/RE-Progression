#RE-Progression

Personal log of my reverse engineering progress, thought processes, and lessons learned.  
Progression Focus: beginner → intermediate → advanced RE (Root‑Me, crackmes.one, CTFs).

## Purpose

- Track my progression and thinking while reversing.
- Record dead ends, mistakes.
- Build a searchable knowledge base for future me.

> Notes here can be messy, iterative, and sometimes wrong. They reflect my real learning process.

## Structure

- `rootme/` – Writeups and notes for [Root‑Me](https://www.root-me.org/) challenges.
- `crackmes-one/` – Notes for [crackmes.one](https://crackmes.one/) challenges.
- `templates/` – Templates I reuse for challenges and sessions.

## How I write notes

For each challenge, I try to capture:

- My initial impression and plan.
- What I tried (including failed attempts).
- Where I got stuck and how I got unstuck.
- Key patterns/techniques I learned.

See `templates/challenge-template.md` for my standard layout.

## Tools I use

- Disassemblers/Decompilers: Ghidra, radare2/Cutter
- Debuggers: x64dbg, WinDbg, GDB
- Misc: strings, objdump, readelf, hex editors, file

## Disclaimer

All work here is for educational purposes on practice platforms and my own binaries.  
I avoid posting copyrighted binaries or anything that facilitates cracking real commercial software.
